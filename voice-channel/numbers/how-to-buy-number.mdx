You can buy a phone number through PolyAI without setting up a telephony provider of your own. PolyAI buys the number on Twilio, manages it for you, and routes it to your agent as soon as the purchase finishes. Usage is billed through PolyAI at 1.5¢ per minute.

<Info>
Managing many numbers? The [Agents API](/api-reference/agents/introduction) exposes bulk [import](/api-reference/agents/endpoint/phone-numbers/import-phone-numbers-into-a-project), [get](/api-reference/agents/endpoint/phone-numbers/batch-get-phone-numbers), and [reassign](/api-reference/agents/endpoint/phone-numbers/reassign-a-phone-number-to-a-different-connector) operations.
</Info>

## Buy a number

In Agent Studio, go to **Integrations**, open the **Telephony** tab, click **Add number**, and choose **Buy Twilio number**. Then:

- Pick a **Country**: **United Kingdom**, **United States**, **Canada**, **Australia** or **Ireland**.
- For the United States and Canada, optionally enter a 3-digit **Area code**. PolyAI only searches the area code you enter and doesn't fall back to a nearby one, so leave it blank unless you need a specific region.
- Pick the **Environment** that should answer calls on the number, and optionally a **Variant**.
- Click **Buy number**. The number appears in the **Telephony** table and calls go straight to your agent.

See [Twilio: Buy Twilio number](/integrations/voice/twilio) for the full steps, error messages, and what to do if a purchase fails.

<Note>
On some accounts, buying a number in the **United Kingdom**, **Australia** or **Ireland** needs an approved regulatory bundle first. Agent Studio tells you when this applies. Submit the bundle on the **Voice > Numbers** page. Review usually takes 1 to 7 business days, and one approved bundle covers every project in your account. See [Regulatory approval](/integrations/voice/twilio#regulatory-approval).
</Note>

Sandbox has no phone numbers, so test there over an in-app call instead. See [Environments](/environments-and-versions/environments#channels).

<Tip>Use separate numbers for each environment so test calls never reach Live.</Tip>

## Delete a number

In the **Telephony** table, open the actions menu on the number, click **Delete number**, and confirm. Calls to the number stop reaching your agent and the number is released. You can't undo this.
