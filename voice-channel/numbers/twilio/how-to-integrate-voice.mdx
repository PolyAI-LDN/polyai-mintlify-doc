---
title: 'Phone number integration'
description: 'Route phone numbers from your own Twilio account to your PolyAI agent.'

---

Connect your [Twilio](https://www.twilio.com/) account to route numbers you already own to your PolyAI agent. The numbers stay in your Twilio account. PolyAI uses your Twilio API credentials to create the SIP trunk your account needs to send calls to your agent, so you don't configure SIP addresses, webhooks or TwiML in Twilio yourself.

<Note>
Don't have a Twilio number? You can [buy one through PolyAI](/voice-channel/numbers/how-to-buy-number) instead, without a Twilio account of your own.
</Note>

## Before you start

In the [Twilio Console](https://console.twilio.com/), collect:

- Your **Account SID**
- An **API key** and **API secret** (create a Standard key under **Account > API keys & tokens**)

Also list the Twilio numbers you want to route to PolyAI, and decide which environment each one should serve.

## Connect your account

In Agent Studio, go to **Integrations**, open the **Telephony** tab, click **Add number**, and choose **Connect Twilio account**. Then:

1. Enter your **Api Key**, **API Secret** and **Account SID**, and click **Continue**.
2. Under **Inbound routing**, add your Twilio numbers to each environment in E.164 format (for example, `+441234567890`). Leave **Sandbox** empty, since Sandbox has no channel connections.
3. Click **Connect**. PolyAI creates the SIP trunk in your Twilio account and routes each number to the environment you chose.

See [Twilio: Connect Twilio account](/integrations/voice/twilio) for the full steps.

## Test the connection

Call one of your routed numbers and confirm the call reaches your agent. Review the call in **Conversations**, filtered to Voice, to check it was handled correctly.

## Handoffs

Twilio transfers calls to your human agents with a SIP REFER. You don't choose the handoff method for Twilio. Set up your transfer destinations in **Handoffs**. See [Call handoffs](/voice-channel/handoffs).
