Branches mode is for small teams working in parallel, each person publishing their own isolated changes. Each person works in their own Branch and publishes it onto Live on their own schedule.

## How it works

A Branch is a safe place to work. It is an isolated copy of your agent, branched off Live, living in the [Sandbox](/environments-and-versions/environments) environment alongside everyone else's Branches.

Your Branch saves as you go. Test it at any time by running [simulation tests](/testing/simulation-tests), or by talking to your agent over an in-app call or chat. Your API and app connections work here too. To test on a channel, move the Branch to [Staging](/environments-and-versions/staging).

When you are ready to ship, publish your Branch. It merges onto Live and becomes the newest version in Live's history.

<Tip>
**Publishing merges, it does not replace.** If Live has moved on since you created your Branch, it is marked **Out of sync**. Sync to pull in the latest changes. If you have both edited the same thing, a conflict screen lets you choose which edit to keep. Nothing is silently lost.
</Tip>

Nobody queues behind anyone. One colleague can spend weeks building a new flow in their Branch. Another can open a Branch of their own, update the knowledge base, and publish the same day, without touching the first colleague's work.

The navigation bar at the top shows where you are and allows you to switch between Live and your Branches.

<Warning>
Branches cannot merge into each other. Each one publishes to Live on its own. To split one piece of work across several people, use [Sub-branches mode](/environments-and-versions/sub-branches-mode).
</Warning>

## Make a small change

The everyday case: a knowledge update, a settings change, a behaviour prompt tweak.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a Branch from Live">
    It branches off the current Live version, so you start from what is actually running.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Make your change">
    It saves as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Run [simulation tests](/testing/simulation-tests) on the Branch, or talk to the agent yourself over an in-app call or chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move to Staging (optional)">
    Move the Branch to [Staging](/environments-and-versions/staging) from its actions menu on the Deployments page, then test through the channel you need.

    <Tip>
    Skip this step unless you need to test on a channel.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    The changes in your Branch merge onto Live and become the newest version in Live's history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Nothing else rides along with your change, and nobody else's work is touched.

## Handle an out of sync Branch

This happens whenever a colleague publishes onto Live before you do. Publishing is blocked until you sync.

<Frame caption="Publishing is unavailable until the Branch is synced with Live">
  <img src="/images/deployment/publish-menu-out-of-sync.png" alt="Publish menu on an out of sync Branch, with Publish greyed out and labelled Sync with live first" style={{ maxWidth: '720px', width: '100%', margin: '0 auto', display: 'block' }} />
</Frame>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Your Branch is marked Out of sync">
    This is expected, not an error.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sync">
    This pulls the latest changes from Live into your Branch.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolve any conflicts">
    If you have both edited the same thing, a conflict resolution screen asks which edit to keep.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test, then publish">
    Check the merged result before you publish.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compare your changes

Before you publish, open the **Publish** menu and choose **Compare changes** to see your Branch against Live side by side, and confirm only your intended change is present. You can also compare any two branches, or two versions in Live's history.

<Frame caption="The Publish menu on a Branch">
  <img src="/images/deployment/publish-menu-branch.png" alt="Publish menu showing View branch history, Compare changes with live, Move to staging, Publish, Publish as an A/B test and Delete branch" style={{ maxWidth: '720px', width: '100%', margin: '0 auto', display: 'block' }} />
</Frame>

For how to read a comparison, see [Compare changes](/environments-and-versions/diffs).

## Roll back Live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Live's version history">
    Every publish is a version there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a previous version and roll back">
    Live returns to that version in one step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Because publishing merges rather than replaces, a rollback cannot be silently undone by someone else's publish.
</Tip>
