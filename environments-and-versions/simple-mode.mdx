Simple mode is for a single builder making straightforward changes. There are no Branches. You edit your agent's working copy in the Sandbox environment, review and test it, then publish it onto Live.

<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/deployment/deployment-simple-dark.svg" alt="Simple mode: a working copy in Sandbox publishes onto Live" style={{ maxWidth: '920px', width: '100%', margin: '0 auto' }} />

## How it works

Your working copy lives in the Sandbox environment, with the API and app connections configured for Sandbox behind it. It saves as you go. Test it at any time by running [simulation tests](/testing/simulation-tests), or by talking to your agent over an in-app call or chat. When you are ready to ship, publish your changes. They merge onto Live and become the newest version in Live's history. If anything goes wrong, roll Live back to any previous version.

## Make a change

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make your change">
    It saves as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Run [simulation tests](/testing/simulation-tests), or talk to the agent yourself over an in-app call or chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Your changes merge onto Live and become the newest version in Live's history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Compare your changes

Before you publish, open the **Publish** menu and choose **Compare changes** to see your working copy against Live side by side, so you can confirm only your intended change is present. The same menu holds **View history** and **Discard changes**.

For how to read a comparison, see [Compare changes](/environments-and-versions/diffs).

## Roll back Live

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Live's version history">
    Every publish is a version there.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a previous version and roll back">
    Live returns to that version in one step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Because publishing merges rather than replaces, a rollback cannot be silently undone by someone else's publish.
</Tip>

<Note>
More than one person building in this project? Switch to [Branches](/environments-and-versions/branches-mode) or [Sub-branches](/environments-and-versions/sub-branches-mode) under **Deployment settings**. Switching never deletes existing work.
</Note>
