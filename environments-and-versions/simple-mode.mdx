Simple mode is the default for new projects, and the right fit when one person is making changes. You edit a **copy** of your agent, test it, and publish it. Customers never see the copy until you publish.

<img className="hidden dark:block" src="/images/deployment/deployment-simple-dark.svg" alt="Simple mode: a copy in Sandbox publishes onto Live" style={{ maxWidth: '920px', width: '100%', margin: '0 auto' }} />

## How it works

Your copy lives in [Sandbox](/environments-and-versions/environments), the environment where you build and test. It saves as you go, so there is nothing to remember to save. When you publish, your changes are added onto Live and become the newest version in Live's history.

There is one copy and one publish at a time. If you need several people working on separate changes at once, switch to [Branches mode](/environments-and-versions/branches-mode) under **Settings** on the Deployments page.

## Make a change

<Steps>
  <Step title="Make your change">
    Edit your agent as normal. The copy saves as you go.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Test it">
    Run [simulation tests](/testing/simulation-tests), or talk to the agent yourself over an in-app call or chat.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Your changes are added onto Live and become the newest version in Live's history.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Check what will change before you publish

Open the **Publish** menu and choose **Compare changes** to see your copy against Live side by side. Use it to confirm that only the change you intended is there. The same menu holds **View history** and **Discard changes**, which throws the copy away and starts again from Live.

For how to read a comparison, see [Compare changes](/environments-and-versions/diffs).

## Undo a publish

If a change goes wrong after publishing, put an earlier version of Live back. See [Roll back a change](/environments-and-versions/introduction#roll-back-a-change).
