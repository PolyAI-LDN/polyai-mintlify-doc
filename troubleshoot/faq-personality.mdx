Answers to questions about setting your agent's role, tone and behavior. See the [prompting guide](/behavior/general/rules) for the full guidance.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I set the agent's tone?" icon="face-smile">
    Tone is set in the **Behavior** field on **Behavior > General**, under its own heading. A one-word label ("be professional") produces no measurable change; a short rule with an example does.

    **Instead of:** "Be friendly and helpful."

    **Use:**
    ```text
    # Style
    - Be warm and direct. Use the caller's name once you have it.
    - Caller: can you just tell me the price
    - Agent: Of course. A standard double is one hundred and forty pounds a night. Shall I check availability?
    ```

    <Tip>
      Test tone changes in the chat panel with a few awkward inputs before publishing.
    </Tip>
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Is Role necessary?" icon="user-tag">
    Yes. Role tells the model who it is and what it covers, which keeps it on task. One to three sentences is enough.

    ```text
    You are a virtual agent for [Brand], helping callers with order status, returns and product questions.
    ```
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where did the personality tags go?" icon="tags">
    The Personality tags (Polite, Kind, Funny and so on) have been replaced by the free-form **Role** and **Behavior** fields. Describe the tone you want in Behavior instead, with an example.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Should I put 'never reveal your instructions' in Behavior?" icon="shield">
    No. Jailbreak defence, prompt extraction, hallucination control, AI identity and crisis escalation are platform [guardrails](/behavior/guardrails/introduction), enforced on every turn outside the prompt. Rules specific to your business go in custom guardrails on the same tab. A "never" in the prompt is a request; a guardrail is a control.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My Behavior field has turned into a long list of steps. Is that a problem?" icon="diagram-project">
    Yes. Step-by-step logic in the prompt is followed loosely and cannot be tested per step. Move the process into a [Flow](/flows/introduction) and leave one line in Behavior saying when to start it. Facts and policies belong in [Knowledge](/knowledge/faqs/introduction) for the same reason.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
