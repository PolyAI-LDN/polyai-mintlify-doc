---
title: "Search conversations"
openapi: "POST /api/v1/conversations/search"
---

<Note>
Free-text metric values are PII. Without the **PII read** permission on your key the response still succeeds, but `call_summary`, `email_subject` and the four `poly_score_*_summary` fields are left out of each conversation's `metrics` map.
</Note>

Filter on any metric from [List metrics](/api-reference/data/endpoint/metrics/list-metrics), plus two special names: `conversation_id` (`eq` or `in`, at most 100 IDs) to fetch specific conversations' metrics, and `CUSTOM_SCORE_<id>` to filter on an AI Score's 1 to 5 value. `poly_score` and `poly_score_normalized` both resolve to the normalised score.
