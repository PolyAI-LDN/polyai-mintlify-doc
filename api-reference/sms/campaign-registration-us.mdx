## Overview

In the US, any business that sends SMS or RCS messages to consumers over standard 10-digit long code (10DLC) numbers must register their brand and each messaging use case (campaign) with **The Campaign Registry (TCR)**. Carriers use this information to filter unregistered or non-compliant traffic, which means unregistered senders see high failure rates, throttling, and eventually blocking.

PolyAI provisions SMS and RCS through Twilio. Registration is coordinated with your PolyAI representative, but the underlying compliance obligations sit with **your business as the brand owner**.

<Info>
  Registration applies to messages sent to US phone numbers, regardless of where your business is based. Non-US brands sending to US consumers must still register.
</Info>

## What needs to be registered

There are two things to register with TCR:

1. **Brand.** Your legal business entity. Registered once per business.
2. **Campaign.** Each distinct messaging use case (for example, appointment reminders, customer support, marketing). A brand can have multiple campaigns.

RCS senders are registered separately with Google through Twilio, but the underlying brand information and consumer consent requirements are the same as 10DLC.

## Information you'll need to provide

To register your brand, PolyAI (via Twilio) will need:

- **Legal business name** exactly as registered.
- **Business type** (for example, private for-profit, public for-profit, non-profit, government).
- **Country of registration.**
- **Tax ID / EIN** (or equivalent for non-US entities, such as a DUNS or LEI number).
- **Registered business address.**
- **Business website.**
- **Vertical / industry** (for example, healthcare, retail, financial services).
- **Authorised representative contact** (name, email, phone).

For each campaign, you'll also need:

- **Campaign use case** (for example, 2-factor authentication, customer care, marketing, mixed).
- **Campaign description.** A short description of what the campaign does.
- **Sample messages.** 2 to 5 example messages that a consumer would receive.
- **Opt-in method and evidence.** How consumers agree to receive messages (web form, checkbox, verbal opt-in on a call, keyword). You must be able to show this if a carrier audits the campaign.
- **Opt-in confirmation message.** The message sent immediately after a consumer opts in.
- **Opt-out language.** Confirmation that `STOP`, `HELP`, and similar keywords are supported and honoured.
- **Privacy policy URL.** A public page describing how consumer data is used. Must state that consumer opt-in data is **not** shared with third parties for marketing.
- **Terms of service URL.** A public page describing the messaging program.
- **Estimated message volume.**

<Tip>
  Your privacy policy and terms of service must be live on a publicly reachable HTTPS URL before you submit the campaign. Carriers spot-check these links during review.
</Tip>

## Consent requirements

US carriers and TCR require **prior express consent** from the consumer before you send any non-emergency SMS or RCS message. The rules are stricter for marketing content (which requires prior express _written_ consent).

At a minimum, your opt-in flow must:

- Clearly identify **your business** as the sender.
- Describe **what kind of messages** the consumer will receive (for example, appointment reminders, order updates).
- Disclose that **message and data rates may apply**.
- Disclose **message frequency** (for example, "up to 4 messages per month").
- Link to your **privacy policy** and **terms of service**.
- Support `STOP` to opt out and `HELP` for support at any time.

See also [Disclaimers and Privacy Policies](/messaging-channel/advanced/disclaimers-and-privacy-policies) for how PolyAI surfaces consent messaging in web chat, which uses the same underlying principles.

## Prohibited content (SHAFT and related)

US carriers block or heavily restrict certain content categories regardless of consent. Campaigns involving any of the following will either be rejected or require special approval:

- **S**ex, hate, alcohol, firearms, tobacco (SHAFT).
- Cannabis and CBD.
- Gambling.
- Loans (particularly high-interest / payday).
- Debt collection and debt forgiveness.
- Cryptocurrency.
- Prescription drugs and controlled substances.

If your use case touches any of these areas, flag it to your PolyAI representative early so registration can be planned accordingly.

## Timelines

Registration is not instant. Typical timelines are:

- **Brand vetting:** 1 to 3 business days.
- **Campaign review:** 1 to 4 weeks, depending on the use case and carrier.
- **RCS sender approval (Google):** 1 to 4 weeks after Twilio submission.

Plan campaign registration alongside your agent build so that go-live isn't blocked waiting on carrier approval.

## Ongoing obligations

Once registered, your business must:

- Keep opt-in records for **at least 4 years** and produce them on request.
- Honour `STOP` requests immediately and permanently, per number and per campaign.
- Notify your PolyAI representative if the campaign's use case, sample messages, opt-in flow, or brand details change materially. Undeclared changes can trigger deregistration.
- Keep your privacy policy and terms of service pages live and up to date.
