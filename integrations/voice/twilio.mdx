Agent Studio gives you two ways to use Twilio. Both start in the same place:

1. Open your project in Agent Studio and go to **Integrations**.
2. Open the **Telephony** tab and click **Add number**.
3. In the **Add a number** panel, under **Transfer or buy a number**, choose a Twilio option.

| Option | Who owns the number | Use it when |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Buy Twilio number** | PolyAI buys and manages the number for you. | You need a new number and don't want to manage a Twilio account. |
| **Connect Twilio account** | The number stays in your own Twilio account. | You already own Twilio numbers that callers know. |

<Note>
If an option shows **Coming soon**, contact your PolyAI account manager.
</Note>

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Buy Twilio number">

PolyAI buys the number, manages it, and routes it to your agent. Calls reach the agent as soon as the number is bought. Usage is billed through PolyAI at 1.5¢ per minute.

## Buy a number

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the buy panel">
    In the **Add a number** panel, click **Buy Twilio number**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a country">
    Pick a **Country**: **United Kingdom**, **United States**, **Canada**, **Australia** or **Ireland**.

    Some countries need regulatory approval before you can buy. See [Regulatory approval](#regulatory-approval) below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="(Optional) Enter an area code">
    For **United States** and **Canada**, an **Area code** field appears. Enter three digits, such as `415`.

    PolyAI only searches the area code you enter. It does not fall back to a nearby one. If that area code is sold out, the purchase fails. Leave the field blank to take any available number in the country.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose an environment">
    Pick the **Environment** that should answer calls on this number. This field is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="(Optional) Choose a variant">
    If your agent has variants, a **Variant** field appears. Pick one to send calls on this number to that variant. Leave it as **Default (no variant)** to use the default agent.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy the number">
    Click **Buy number**. When the purchase finishes, the panel closes and the number appears in the **Telephony** table with its environment and variant. Calls to it go straight to your agent.

    If the purchase fails, Agent Studio shows **Couldn't buy the number** with the reason. Try again, or try without an area code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Use separate numbers for each environment so test calls never reach Live.
</Tip>

## Regulatory approval

On some accounts, local regulations require an approved regulatory bundle before you can buy a number in the **United Kingdom**, **Australia** or **Ireland**. If this applies to you, a warning appears after you choose the country, and **Buy number** stays disabled.

| Message | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **[Country] needs regulatory approval** | No approved bundle exists for this country yet. | Click **Go to Numbers**, submit your bundle on the **Voice > Numbers** page, then come back. |
| **[Country] approval is under review** | Your bundle is submitted and waiting for approval. | Wait. Review usually takes 1 to 7 business days. You can buy once it's approved. |
| **Couldn't check regulatory approval for [Country]** | Agent Studio couldn't check your bundle status. | Click **Try again**. Buying stays blocked until the check succeeds. |

A bundle covers every project in your account, so you only submit it once per country.

<Note>
If your account is on a trial that can't go live yet, the panel shows an upgrade prompt instead of the form.
</Note>

## Delete a bought number

1. In the **Telephony** table, open the actions menu on the number.
2. Click **Delete number** and confirm.

Calls to the number stop reaching your agent and the number is released. You can't undo this.

  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Connect Twilio account">

Use phone numbers you already own. They stay in your Twilio account. You bring your Twilio API credentials and your numbers. PolyAI uses the credentials to create the SIP trunks your Twilio account needs to send calls to your agent. You don't write TwiML, raise a PolyAI ticket, or negotiate a shared SIP trunk.

<Warning>
Only one telephony integration can be connected to a project at a time. If the project already has one, the **Connect Twilio account** option is disabled, and the card shows why:

- **Twilio is already connected to this project.**
- **Only one telephony integration can be connected to a project at a time, and this project already has one.**
</Warning>

## Before you start

In the [Twilio Console](https://console.twilio.com/), collect:

- **Account SID**: shown in the **Account Info** panel on the console dashboard.
- **API key and API secret**: go to **Account > API keys & tokens** and click **Create API key**. Use a Standard key. Twilio shows the secret only once, so copy it before you close the dialog.

Also make a list of the Twilio numbers you want to route to PolyAI, and decide which environment each one should serve.

## Connect your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Twilio setup">
    In the **Add a number** panel, click **Connect Twilio account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your Twilio credentials">
    On **Step 1: Twilio account credentials**, fill in all three fields:

    - **Api Key**
    - **API Secret**
    - **Account SID**

    Click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your numbers to each environment">
    On **Step 2: Inbound routing**, add the Twilio numbers that should reach your agent in each environment. You can add more than one number per environment.

    - **Live**: numbers that reach your published agent.
    - **Pre-release** (shown as **Staging** in some projects): numbers for testing before you go live.
    - **Sandbox**: leave this empty. [Sandbox](/environments-and-versions/environments#sandbox) has no channel connections, so test there with an in-app call instead.

    Enter each number in E.164 format: a `+`, the country code, then the number, with no spaces (for example, `+441234567890`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect">
    Click **Connect**. PolyAI creates the SIP trunk on your Twilio account and routes each number to the environment you chose.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up handoffs">
    After you connect, Agent Studio asks you to **Update handoff configuration**. Click **Take me there** to set up transfers to your human agents now, or **Dismiss** to do it later.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Configure handoffs

Twilio hands calls back to your agents with a SIP REFER. You don't choose the handoff method for Twilio. Set up transfer destinations in **Handoffs**. See [Call handoffs](/voice-channel/handoffs) for the full reference.

## Remove a number

1. In the **Telephony** table, open the actions menu on the number.
2. Click **Delete number** and confirm.

Calls to the number stop reaching your agent. You can't undo this.
