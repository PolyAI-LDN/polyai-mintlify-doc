Wren connects to ChatGPT as an app. Once it's set up, your team can ask Wren about their PolyAI agents, and have it make changes, without leaving ChatGPT.

<Info>
Wren for ChatGPT is in early access. Ask your PolyAI representative to turn it on for your account. It's available in the US today. In the UK or EU, contact your PolyAI representative.
</Info>

## Before you start

<CardGroup cols={3}>
  <Card title="ChatGPT workspace admin" icon="user-shield">
    You need to be an admin or owner of a ChatGPT Business, Enterprise or Edu workspace to add an app for your team.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Client ID and secret" icon="key">
    Your PolyAI team sends these to you separately. Keep the secret private.
  </Card>
  <Card title="PolyAI logins" icon="right-to-bracket">
    Each person who uses Wren signs in with the login they use for Agent Studio. Their email address must be verified.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Set up Wren

Setup takes about ten minutes, all in ChatGPT.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn on developer mode">
    In ChatGPT, open **Workspace settings** and turn on **Developer mode** for yourself. ChatGPT only lets admins and owners with developer mode on create apps.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create the app">
    Go to **Workspace settings** › **Apps** › **Create**. Name the app **Wren**, then enter the MCP server URL for the Agent Studio you sign in to:

    | You sign in to Agent Studio at | MCP server URL |
    |---|---|
    | `studio.us.poly.ai` | `https://api.us.poly.ai/v1/mcp/wren` |
    | `studio.poly.ai` | `https://api.studio.poly.ai/v1/mcp/wren` |

    Choose **OAuth** as the authentication method.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set up sign-in">
    Under **Client registration**, choose **User-Defined OAuth Client** and fill in:

    | Field | Value |
    |---|---|
    | OAuth Client ID | From your PolyAI team |
    | OAuth Client Secret | From your PolyAI team |
    | Token endpoint auth method | `client_secret_post` |

    Leave the callback URL as ChatGPT shows it. ChatGPT also notes that CIMD is unavailable. That's expected, and you can ignore it.

    <Warning>
    Fill in the client secret even though ChatGPT marks it optional. Without it, sign-in fails.
    </Warning>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Connect and check the tools">
    Select **Create**, then **Connect**. Sign in with your Agent Studio login. ChatGPT then lists Wren's tools.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Share Wren with your team">
    The app starts as a draft that only you can use. Go to **Workspace settings** › **Apps** › **Drafts**, review it, then select **Publish** to make it available to your workspace.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Try it">
    Start a chat, select **+**, choose **Wren**, and ask about one of your agents, for example *"Which of my Agent Studio projects can Wren work on?"* The first time, you're asked to sign in with your Agent Studio login.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Wren can do in ChatGPT

- **Find your projects.** Ask by name, and Wren finds the right account and project.
- **Explain an agent.** Ask how it behaves, why a call went wrong, or what a change would involve. Wren reads the project without changing anything.
- **Make changes.** Wren builds, edits, fixes and tests on a branch, then reports what it changed.
- **Check on a run.** Long jobs keep running in the background, so you can come back and ask how they're going.
- **Answer Wren's questions.** When Wren needs a decision or wants you to approve its plan, ChatGPT shows you the question.

## Good to know

- **Changes stay on a branch.** Wren never merges or publishes on its own. Review and publish its branch in Agent Studio.
- **Some changes stay in Agent Studio.** Entering secrets or API credentials, and confirming configuration changes, still happen in Agent Studio. Wren tells you when that's needed.
- **Each chat is its own conversation.** Every Wren conversation also appears in Agent Studio, with a link in Wren's replies.
