The **October 2026** PolyAI release introduces **AI Scores**: 1–5 conversation scores that you define, graded by AI against your own criteria.

<Accordion title="AI Scores: score conversations against your own criteria" icon="list-check" id="ai-scores">

**Rollout date:** October 2026. Available on every project.

[PolyScore](/analytics/polyscore) measures conversation quality the same way for every agent. **AI Scores** let you measure what matters to your business, such as compliance with a policy, accuracy, or a specific outcome.

![Conversations table with two AI Score columns, and the breakdown tooltip open on one score showing each criterion's weight and answer](/images/analytics/ai-scores-table.png)

**How it works**

- Give the score a name and a description, and choose the channels it applies to: voice, webchat, or both.
- Add up to five criteria. Each criterion is graded **Good / Fair / Poor** or **Yes / No**, and has a weight. Weights add up to 100%.
- Optionally, add **exclusion criteria** to skip conversations the score doesn't apply to.
- After each conversation ends, the AI grades every criterion from the transcript. The criteria combine into a 1–5 score, to one decimal place.
- Start from scratch or from one of six templates, including **Simplified PolyScore**, **Compliance check**, and **User effort**.

**Where you see AI Scores**

- **Conversations** — one column per active score, with the criteria breakdown on hover, a filter, and a section on the **Scores** tab.
- **CSV export** — one **AI Score: &lt;score name&gt;** column per score.
- **Dashboards** — chart AI Scores on self-serve dashboards. Look under **AI Scores created by you** in the **Metric** field.
- **Wren** — ask about AI Score averages and trends.

**Limits and access**

- Up to 10 AI Scores per project, with up to 3 active at a time.
- Only Admins can create and manage AI Scores. Anyone with **Read** on **Conversations** can see the results.
- AI Scores apply to new conversations only. Turning a score on or editing it doesn't rescore past conversations.

PolyScore is unchanged and keeps running alongside AI Scores.

To get started, go to **Analyze > Analytics**, select **Edit**, then select **AI scores**. See [AI Scores](/analytics/ai-scores) for the full guide.
